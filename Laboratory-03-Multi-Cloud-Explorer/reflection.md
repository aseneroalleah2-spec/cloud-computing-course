# Mission Reflection

Mission 3 gave me a better understanding of the three major cloud platforms: AWS, Microsoft Azure, and Google Cloud Platform. Before completing this activity, I knew that these platforms were used for cloud computing, but I did not fully understand how they were different from one another. Through my research, I learned that each platform has its own features, services, advantages, and areas where it performs well.

Among the three platforms, Microsoft Azure stood out to me the most because of its benefits for students. The free monthly allowances and credits can help students practice using cloud services without spending too much money. I think this is useful for students who are still learning about cloud computing and want to gain practical experience.

I also learned that AWS, Azure, and Google Cloud have many similar services, such as virtual machines, storage, databases, networking, and identity management. However, they have different strengths. AWS is known for its wide range of services, Azure is a strong choice for organizations using Microsoft technologies, and Google Cloud is well suited for Artificial Intelligence, data analytics, and Kubernetes.

The activity taught me that choosing a cloud platform depends on the needs of the organization. Factors such as budget, existing systems, performance, scalability, security, and business goals should be considered before making a decision. There is no cloud provider that is automatically the best for every company.

Overall, Mission 3 improved my knowledge of cloud computing and helped me understand how cloud services are used in real-world organizations. It also helped me improve my GitHub Cloud Computing Portfolio by organizing my research, comparisons, recommendations, screenshots, and other activities. This experience gave me more confidence in understanding and comparing cloud platforms.
